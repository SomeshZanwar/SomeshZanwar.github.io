The Governance Stack

Two connected projects. One unified question: can this agent use this data right now? The first project governs the data, validating quality, tracking metadata, and flagging failures before they reach reporting. The second governs the agent, enforcing authorization, checking dataset trustworthiness at the time of access, and logging every decision to a unified audit trail.
